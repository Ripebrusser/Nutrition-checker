## Calibration Process

### Procedure

1. **Independent Review:** Primary Reviewer and Quality Auditor each independently review all 5 calibration units in the workbench.
2. **Decision Recording:** Each reviewer records for each unit:
   - Mapping decision
   - Rationale (≥50 characters)
   - Evidence references
   - Confidence (1-5)
3. **Comparison:** Calibration coordinator compares decisions.
4. **Discrepancy Resolution:** Any discrepancies discussed and resolved.
5. **Consensus:** Both reviewers agree on final decision for all 5 units.
6. **Documentation:** Results recorded in this document.

### Independence Requirement

- Reviewers complete calibration **independently** (no discussion during review)
- Reviews conducted in separate sessions (can be same day, separate sessions)
- No sharing of decisions/rationales until both complete
